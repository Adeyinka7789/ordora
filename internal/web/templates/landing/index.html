FAQ

Questions before you switch

Do my customers need to download an app or create an account? expand_more

No. Customers open a private link that works in any browser. They see the order status, photos, and balance — no login, no app, no friction.

How are payments tracked? expand_more

Every payment is a separate record. Partial payments, deposits, and final settlements all roll up into the order's balance automatically. Attach receipts and they stay with the payment forever.

Can I customize order stages for my workflow? expand_more

V1 ships with a fixed lifecycle: NEW → CONFIRMED → IN PROGRESS → READY → OUT FOR DELIVERY → DELIVERED → COMPLETED. Configurable workflows arrive in a future release.

Is my business data isolated from other businesses? expand_more

Yes — every business is isolated at the database level with row-level security. Your data can never be seen by another business, by design. See our security page for details.
