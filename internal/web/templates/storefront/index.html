How it works

  1. 1

    Share your link. The customer opens your form and tells you what they need — name, contact, description, date, budget.

  2. 2

    You get notified. Every submission lands in your orders list as NEW, and you get an in-app notification.

  3. 3

    Confirm and track. Review the request, add items and pricing, then share the order's tracking link so the customer can follow progress and download their receipt.
